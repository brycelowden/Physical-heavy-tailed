Code and data used to perform the analyses in the paper "Branching neurons produce strong connections" by Bryce Lowden, Aanjaneya Kumar, Jose M. Betancourt, Damon A. Clark, and Christopher W. Lynn.

(1) The directed FAFB connectivity CSV can be downloaded from FlyWire: https://codex.flywire.ai/api/download?dataset=fafb. Use 'FlyWireDirToUnd.py' to generate the undirected connectivity CSV.

(2) The FAFB SWC neuron skeletons can be downloaded from FlyWire: https://codex.flywire.ai/api/download?dataset=fafb.

Dorkenwald, S. et al. Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain. Nature 634, 124–138 (2024).

Schlegel, P. et al. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of drosophila. Nature 634, 139–152 (2024).

(3) The periphery data used in 'Fig5_NeuronSorting.py' can be obtained from (Jose Preprint).

(4) The directed BANC connectivity CSV can be downloaded from FlyWire: https://codex.flywire.ai/api/download?dataset=banc. Use 'UndirectedBANC.py' in NonFAFB to generate the undirected connectivity CSV.

Bates, A. S. et al. Distributed control circuits across a brain-and-cord connectome. Preprint: biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.31.667571v3 (2025).

(5) The directed MANC connectivity CSV can be downloaded from FlyWire: https://codex.flywire.ai/api/download?dataset=manc. Use 'UndirectedMANC.py' in NonFAFB to generate the undirected connectivity CSV.

Takemura, S. et al. A connectome of the male drosophila ventral nerve cord. Elife (2024).

(6) The directed Larval Drosophila connectome CSV comes from Winding, M. et al. The connectome of an insect brain. Science 379 (2023).

(7) The directed C. Elegans connectivity CSV comes from Varshney, L. R., Chen, B. L., Paniagua, E., Hall, D. H. & Chklovskii, D. B. Structural properties of the caenorhabditis elegans neuronal network. PLoS Computational Biology 7 (2011). Use 'UndirectedCelegans.py' in NonFAFB to generate the undirected connectivity CSV.
